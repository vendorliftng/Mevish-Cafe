Digital Operations & Training Portal

Before taking any orders, the system must be calibrated for the current shift to ensure accurate financial tracking.

Pre-Shift Checklist

  • Open the Kitchen Dashboard (mevish.com.ng/dashboard).
  • Ensure the tablet has an active internet connection.
  • Click the top-right dropdown and select your specific Cashier Name.
  • Verify the POS machine is on and connected.
⚠️ Manager Note: If a new staff member is hired, add their name to the "Cashiers" tab in the Google Sheet. The system will update automatically.

Speed and accuracy are vital. The dashboard acts as the single source of truth for the kitchen.

Accepting an Order

  • Wait for a new Yellow (Pending) order to appear.
  • Read the "Special Instructions" carefully.
  • Click the red [15m], [30m], or [45m] button to accept the ticket.
  • Note: Clicking this starts the live countdown on the customer's phone!

Marking as Ready

  • Once plated, click the gold [Mark as Ready] button.
  • Serve the food to the correct table number listed on the ticket.

Accurate payment tracking ensures the end-of-day analytics are flawless.

Closing a Ticket

  • Only close a ticket AFTER the customer has paid.
  • If paid via Terminal, click the green [POS] button.
  • If paid via physical money, click the blue [Cash] button.

Handling Mistakes (Voids)

If an order is a duplicate, fake, or cancelled by the customer, you must clear it to keep the screen clean.

  • Locate the incorrect order.
  • Click the black [ ❌ ] button to Void it.
  • The order will vanish from the kitchen but be saved in the Manager's Audit Log.

The system automatically calculates all math. No manual calculators required.

Closing the Restaurant

  • Open the Analytics page (mevish.com.ng/manager).
  • Look at "Total Cash Expected" and count the physical register drawer to ensure it matches.
  • Look at "Total POS Settled" and verify it matches your terminal's End-of-Day receipt.
  • Review the "Cancelled Orders" audit log at the bottom to check for any suspicious voids.
